Graduate Students

The Mirzaei Lab is always interested in hearing from highly motivated individuals who are passionate about cancer research and interested in pursuing graduate training in a collaborative and multidisciplinary research environment.

Prospective graduate students with interests in cancer biology, cancer immunology, tumor–immune interactions, functional genomics, CRISPR-based approaches, and related areas are encouraged to contact us.

Interested applicants should email rmirzaei@ualberta.ca with a CV and academic transcript. Please also include a brief description of your research interests and why you are interested in joining the Mirzaei Lab.
